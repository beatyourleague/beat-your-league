1. What arrives, and when

  • Today: your first report. Before the season starts that is your roster report — every bye week on your team, last season's numbers under your scoring with each player's rank at his position, and the weeks your lineup cannot be filled. During the season, join before that week's first game and you get the current week's report instead; join after it and the roster report comes today, the week's report on Tuesday.
  • Every Tuesday morning after that: the week's report — the lineup we would set for your exact roster under your scoring, every close call decided, the odds wherever both players are confirmed to play, and a plain reason wherever there aren't any.
  • Saturday, only when it matters: if Friday's injury report changes your lineup, a short final check tells you the swap. No news, no email.
  • A separate email in a moment: a short receipt from us with your renewal terms and the cancel link, to keep.

2. If your roster changes

Rosters churn — that is the point of the waiver run. Reply to any report with your updated roster and the next one follows it.

3. Changed your mind

Full refund, no questions, any time until your second weekly report's week kicks off — one per person (the exact terms). And you can cancel yourself in about a minute from your billing page, whose link is at the bottom of every report we send; the steps are here. We do not ask why, and there is no retention call.
