1. What arrives, and when

  • Today: your first report. Before the season starts that is your roster report — every bye week on your team, last season's numbers under your scoring with each player's rank at his position, and the weeks your lineup cannot be filled. From Week 2 on, join at least an hour before that week's first game and you get the current week's report instead; join later, or in Week 1, and the roster report comes today, the week's report on Tuesday.
  • Every Tuesday morning after that: the week's report — your lineup decided under your scoring, every close call explained, and a plain reason wherever a row has no odds.
  • Saturday, only when it matters: if Friday's injury report changes your lineup, a short final check tells you the swap — and if a starting spot still has nobody to fill it, it says so. Nothing to change, no email.
  • A separate email, usually within the hour: a short receipt from us with your terms, to keep. Your pass was one payment: nothing renews.

2. If your roster changes

Rosters churn — that is the point of the waiver run. Use the roster link at the bottom of any weekly report, or reply to one with your updated roster. We email you a button to confirm; press it and your next report uses the new roster — and Saturday's final check too, if you press it by Friday night (ET).

3. Changed your mind

Full refund, no questions, any time until your second weekly report's week kicks off (or a week after your last report, if the season ends first) — one per person (the exact terms). Just reply to any of our emails to ask — nobody contacts you to change your mind. There is nothing to cancel: your pass was one payment and ends on its own after the season's last report. To stop the emails before then, reply "stop" (details).

4. Anything else

Email hello@beatyourleague.com — refunds, deletion, or anything that looks wrong. The full Terms and Privacy Policy are always one click away.
