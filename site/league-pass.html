Why one manager buys it for twelve

It levels the field

One manager buying an edge is an arms race. Everyone having the same tool is just a better league — and it kills the "he only won because he pays for stuff" argument before anyone makes it.

It feeds the group chat

Twelve managers making calls from their own reports on Tuesday, and a group chat finding out on Sunday who's held up. The screenshots, the arguments and the rematches all get louder.

Nobody's team gets shared

Each report goes only to the manager it's about. We never share one manager's report with another, and we never publish your league's names anywhere.

See the report each manager gets · How it works

Any manager in the league can buy it; you don't have to be the commissioner. Managers who never sign up simply don't get a report, and there's no charge either way. Leagues of 8, 10, 12 or 14 teams are all covered.

What you do after paying

  1. Pay once, on Stripe. Your own roster goes in at the same time, so your report is the first one.
  2. Text the league the link. Your welcome email carries your league's signup link. Send it to the group chat with the email you paid with, which is how each seat is matched to your pass.
  3. Each manager pastes their roster. About a minute, nothing to pay, and their own report lands the next Tuesday.
