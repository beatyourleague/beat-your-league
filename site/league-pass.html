What you do after paying

  1. Pay once, on Stripe. Your own roster goes in at the same time, so your report is the first one.
  2. Send the league your link. Your welcome email carries your league's seat link, and so do your weekly reports. It needs only their roster, and it's the only way to claim a seat — nobody can take one by typing your email.
  3. Each manager pastes their roster. About a minute, nothing to pay, and their first report lands the day they confirm their seat (we email them a button), then every Tuesday.

Before you ask

Can a manager join late?
Yes, any time during the season, with your league's link. Their first report lands the day they confirm their seat (we email them a button), then every Tuesday.
How many seats does a pass cover?
As many as your league has teams — one for every other manager, plus one to spare for a co-manager. Seats go to the managers who confirm theirs first; anyone who claims after they're all taken is told so by email.
What if a manager leaves or is replaced?
The new manager claims a seat with your league's link. If every seat is taken, theirs frees up when the one who left replies "stop" to any of their reports: they're off within 10 business days, and their seat is released. Your pass isn't affected.
We co-manage a team. Do we both get it?
Yes. Each co-manager claims a seat with their own email and the same roster, and each gets the report, while your league's seats last.
I'm in two leagues that both have a pass. What do I do?
Claim a seat in each, with each league's own link. Each seat is its own report, built from that league's roster and settings.
I already bought my own pass. Can I switch to the League Pass?
Yes: buy the League Pass with the same email and the same roster. Your League Pass covers your own team, so you still get one report a week, not two. Both are one payment, so nothing keeps billing; if you'd rather not have paid twice, reply to any report while your Rest-of-Season Pass's refund window is open and we'll refund it.
What are my leaguemates' emails used for?
Only to send each manager their own report, the email confirming their seat, and — if they ask — the confirmation of a roster change. Nobody sees another manager's report or address: your own report shows how many seats are claimed, as a count, never who. If that count shows seats you don't recognize, reply "reset seats": we send you a new league link and release every seat claimed before it.
What happens to the seats if I take a refund?
Your league's seats last through the season with your pass: it's one payment with nothing to cancel, and if you take a refund, every seat's reports stop too.
