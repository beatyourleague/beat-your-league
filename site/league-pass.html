Why one manager buys it for twelve

It levels the field

One manager buying an edge is an arms race. Everyone having the same tool is just a better league — and it kills the "he only won because he pays for stuff" argument before anyone makes it.

It feeds the group chat

Twelve managers making calls from their own reports on Tuesday, and a group chat finding out on Sunday who's held up. The screenshots, the arguments and the rematches all get louder.

Nobody's team gets shared

Each report goes only to the manager it's about. We never share one manager's report with another, and we never publish your league's names anywhere.

Before you ask

Can a manager join late?
Yes, any time the pass is active, with your league's link. Their first report lands the day they confirm their seat (we email them a button) — that week's lineup if it's Week 2 or later and the week's games haven't started, otherwise their roster report — then every Tuesday.
How many seats does a pass cover?
As many as your league has teams — one for every other manager, plus one to spare for a co-manager. Seats go to the managers who confirm theirs first; anyone who claims after they're all taken is told so by email.
What if a manager leaves or is replaced?
The new manager claims a seat with your league's link. The one who left can stop their reports by replying "stop" to any of them, and they're off within 10 business days. Your pass isn't affected.
We co-manage a team. Do we both get it?
Yes. Each co-manager claims a seat with their own email and the same roster, and each gets the report, while your league's seats last.
I'm in two leagues that both have a pass. What do I do?
Claim a seat in each, with each league's own link. Each seat is its own report, built from that league's roster and settings.
I already have my own season pass or monthly plan. Can I switch to the League Pass?
Yes: buy the League Pass with the same email and the same roster. Your League Pass covers your own team, so you still get one report a week, not two. Your other subscription doesn't stop on its own: cancel it from your billing page if you don't want it to bill again. We don't cancel or refund anything on our own.
What are my leaguemates' emails used for?
Only to send each manager their own report, the email confirming their seat, and — if they ask — the confirmation of a roster change. Nobody sees another manager's report or address: your own report shows how many seats are claimed, as a count, never who.
What happens to the seats if I cancel or take a refund?
Your league's seats last as long as your pass does: if you cancel it or take a refund, every seat's reports stop too.

Fair play

Everyone gets the same report, built for their own roster, so the league gets sharper, not lopsided. A league full of good information still crowns exactly one champion; that part is up to you.
