Why a commissioner buys this

It levels the field

One manager buying an edge is an arms race. Everyone having the same tool is just a better league — and it kills the "he only won because he pays for stuff" argument before anyone makes it.

It makes the group chat

Every manager who signs up gets graded calls on Monday. The receipts get screenshotted, argued with, and thrown back at each other. That's the part people actually remember about a season.

Nobody's team gets shared

Each report goes only to the manager it's about. We never tell your league who picked whom as their rival, and we never publish your league's names anywhere.

It's cheaper than one buy-in
