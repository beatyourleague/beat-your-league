Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 August 2026. The short version: we collect your email, your roster and your league's scoring settings, and nothing else. No cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no advertising. This policy is part of our Terms of Service.

The whole list. Your email address, the roster you entered, and your league's scoring settings — scoring type, league size and lineup shape. That is everything we hold about you. We never see your card, you never create a password with us, and we never connect to your league account.

1. What we collect, and why

  • Your email address — because the product is an email. It is how your report reaches you and how we send the acknowledgment and renewal notices the law requires.
  • The roster you entered and your league's scoring settings — because the report is computed for your exact team under your exact rules. Without them there is no product.
  • A reference to your Stripe customer record and your subscription status — so we know each week who is entitled to a report, and so cancelling actually stops the emails.

We do not build a profile of you, and we do not use your data to train anything.

2. What we never ask for

  • Passwords or logins of any kind. We never connect to your league account on any platform — you type or paste your roster, and that is the whole connection.
  • Payment details. Card numbers go to Stripe on Stripe's own page. We never see them, never transmit them and never store them.
  • Anything we do not need — no postal address, no phone number, no date of birth, no location.

3. Cookies, analytics and tracking

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and carries no advertising or tracking scripts. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to. Two honest details: our pages load type from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google and Google can see your IP address as it would for any site using them; and our host serves the pages, which involves the ordinary server logs any web host keeps.

4. Who else sees your data

Two processors, each doing one job, and neither gets anything beyond it:

  • Stripe handles payment and billing. They hold your payment details and your subscription; we hold only a reference to it.
  • Our email provider delivers the reports. They handle your address and the message.

Each has its own privacy policy. We do not sell your data, rent it, or hand it to advertisers or data brokers — not now and not later. If that ever changed we would have to tell you first, and you could leave.

One thing worth being exact about, because "we only keep three things" could otherwise read as a dodge. Stripe's own dashboard shows us the country your card was issued in and the postal code it checked against — Stripe collects those to verify the card, not because we asked for them, and we use them only to see where we are selling — which matters because tax registration rules depend on it. They stay in Stripe. They are never copied into our records, and the list at the top of this page is the whole of what we hold.

5. Your report is yours alone

It is built from the roster you gave us and public NFL data, and it goes only to you. We never publish your name, your email or your roster anywhere. Our public record of graded calls names NFL players and never subscribers, and carries nothing that could identify you or your league.

6. Where the football data comes from

Openly licensed public NFL data. Nothing about you, your league or your leaguemates is in it, and nothing about you is ever sent to it.

7. How long we keep it

While you are subscribed, and for a short period afterwards for accounting, then we delete it. Ask and we will delete it sooner.

8. Seeing or deleting what we hold

Email hello@beatyourleague.com and we will tell you exactly what we have, or delete it — whichever you ask for. You do not need an account or a form, and we will not ask you why. Cancelling stops the billing; asking us removes the data. If you are covered by the GDPR, the UK GDPR, Canadian privacy law, or a US state privacy law, these are the access, correction, deletion and portability rights those laws give you, and we honour them for everyone regardless of where you live.

9. Age

This is an adult product: you must be 18 or older to subscribe. We do not knowingly collect anything from children, and if you believe a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If a change materially affects you, we will email subscribers before it takes effect, and the date at the top of this page will change.

11. Who to contact

Beat Your League is an independent solo project. Contact hello@beatyourleague.com for anything on this page — access, deletion, or a question about how something works. Billing questions are covered in the Terms of Service.
