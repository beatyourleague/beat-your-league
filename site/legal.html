Terms & Privacy

Short, in plain words, and written to be kept. If anything here conflicts with what a sales page says, this page wins.

What you're buying

A weekly Rival Report for one fantasy football league, emailed to you during the NFL regular season and your league's playoffs. It's analysis built from your league's own public record on Sleeper. It is not betting advice, not a tout service, and not a guarantee of any result. Fantasy decisions are yours.

Price, renewal and cancelling

  • The season pass is $29, charged when you subscribe.
  • It renews once a year at $29 unless you cancel. We email you before it bills.
  • You can cancel yourself at any time from your own Substack account. We will never ask you to contact us to stop a subscription, and we will never add steps to make leaving harder.
  • Payments and cancellation are handled by Substack and its payment processor. We never see or store your card details.

Refunds

Full refund, no questions, any time through Week 2 of your first season pass — one refund per person. If you re-subscribe after taking a refund, that purchase is final. You keep every report we already sent either way.

What we collect, and what we don't

  • What we keep: your email address, the Sleeper league you chose, and which manager you named as your rival. That's the whole list.
  • What we never ask for: your Sleeper password (the data we read is public and needs none), and any payment details.
  • Where league data comes from: Sleeper's public API — the same league record every member of your league can already see. We don't access private messages, accounts, or anything hidden from your leaguemates.
  • Your leaguemates: your report discusses other managers in your league, because that is the product. It goes only to you. We do not publish your league's identities: the public ledger and the sample report use neutral labels.
  • Sharing: we don't sell your data or hand it to advertisers. Email delivery and payment run through Substack, which has its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Deletion: email us and we'll delete your registry entry and unsubscribe you. Cancelling stops billing; asking us removes the data.

Age and eligibility

You must be 18 or older to subscribe. This is a paid analysis newsletter for season-long fantasy football; it involves no wagering, no prize pools, and no money contests.

Honest limits

Projections are estimates and will be wrong sometimes — that is what a probability means. When we can't stand behind a number we leave it out rather than guess, and we publish our misses on the public ledger alongside the hits. Nothing here promises you'll win your league.
