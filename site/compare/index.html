Which one, honestly

You run four or more leaguesFantasyPros. Nothing else manages volume as well, and none of the rest of us are trying to.
You mostly care about draft dayThe Ultimate Draft Kit if you listen to the podcast, Establish The Run if you'd rather read, BeerSheets if you'd rather pay nothing. Our product doesn't even start until your draft is over.
You want a guarantee before you spendDraft Sharks (to Dec 31) or Footballguys (30 days) put real money-back terms in writing. Ours is narrower: no-questions until your first weekly report's week kicks off.
You want the deepest projections operation4for4 — they've built the brand on a public accuracy record, which is the right thing to build one on.
You'd rather not enter your roster by handFantasyPros or 4for4 — both pull your league in with one tap. We can't: we don't connect to league platforms, so you type or paste your players once when you sign up.
You want every weekly call written down and graded in publicThen us. Plenty of the rows above will set your lineup; what you get here is a report that arrives already decided, the odds on every call worth making, and every one of them settled against the real box score on a page anyone can read — misses included. If that's not what you want, one of the rows above is — that's why they're all here.
