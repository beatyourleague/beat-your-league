Is it worth joining mid-season?
Mid-season is when it's at its strongest. Every projection now draws on this season's own games, the odds print wherever both players are playing, off the injury report and have enough games on record (not on team defenses), and every report checks the weeks still ahead, playoff weeks included, for a bye that leaves a starting spot nobody on your roster can fill, while there's still time to cover it. Join before a week's first game and that week's report usually lands within the hour, with a full refund until your second weekly report's week kicks off. Early in a season the odds start later: Week 1 sets your lineup in last season's order with no odds, full-PPR, 12-team standard lineups that start a kicker and a defense get them from Week 2, and every other setup from Week 4 (here's what a Week 1 report looks like).
Does it work with my league?
Yes, on ESPN, Yahoo, Sleeper, CBS or any other app: paste your roster once (or pick names from a list), with no league login and no password. It's scored for full PPR, half-PPR or standard scoring (4-point passing touchdowns), 8, 10, 12 or 14 teams (in between? pick the closest). Lineups: standard (ESPN's and Yahoo's default), two FLEX (Sleeper's default), three WR with a W/R/T FLEX, or superflex, each with or without a kicker and a team defense. Extras like 6-point passing touchdowns or a TE premium get the nearest standard version.
I'm in more than one league. What do I need?
Each team gets its own report and its own pass: start with one league and add another the same way, and one email address can hold several. Each pass is one payment, so nothing renews. Running a whole league? The League Pass covers every manager in a league of up to 14 teams with one payment.
When does it arrive?
Tuesday morning, every week of the season, after Monday night's game and days before your lineup locks. Join before a week's first game (from Week 2) and that week's report usually lands within the hour; otherwise your roster report does, and your first weekly report comes the next Tuesday one goes out. And if Friday's injury report changes your lineup, a short final check lands Saturday with the swap.
What if my roster changes mid-season?
Trades and pickups happen — update your roster from the link in any report, or reply with it. A change confirmed by Friday night (ET) is in that Saturday's check, and every report after it uses it.
Can I check how it decides?
Yes. Every call shows the projection behind it and the player's recent games, so you can look up the box scores yourself. Each report after your first also lists the calls with odds from the one before it, and how each one finished. How each part is worked out is on the How it works page.
Who's behind this?
Beat Your League is independent and small: one fantasy player built it, after too many lineups set at 12:55 on Sunday. It's paid for only by subscribers — no ads, and your data is never sold. Questions go to hello@beatyourleague.com.
