Fair questions

Before you ask the group chat

Does it work with my league app?
Any of them — Sleeper, ESPN, Yahoo, anything. You paste your roster once (or pick names from a list) and we take it from there. No league connection needed.
Does it match my league's scoring?
Yes. PPR, half-PPR, or standard, plus your league size and lineup shape (superflex included). Every number is computed under your settings.
When does it arrive?
Tuesday morning, every week of the season — once the whole week's games are in the books, days before your lineup locks. The first one lands Tuesday, Sep 8.
What if my roster changes mid-season?
Trades and pickups happen — reply to any file with your updated roster and it follows from the next Tuesday.
When do the numbers start?
Week 2 for the standard setup we've graded (full PPR, 12 teams, the usual lineup) — leaning on last season at first, and saying so on each row. Other setups start in Week 4 — which is after the Week-2 refund window closes, so weigh that before you buy.
