Before you ask the group chat

Is it worth joining mid-season?
Yes, it's built for it. Every report checks the byes still ahead, playoff weeks included, and projections draw on this season's games. Join before a week's first game and that week's report usually lands within the hour.
Does it work with my league?
Yes: ESPN, Yahoo, Sleeper, CBS or any app. Paste your roster, no login. Full PPR, half-PPR or standard scoring (4-point passing touchdowns), 8, 10, 12 or 14 teams (in between? pick the closest), and every common lineup: standard (ESPN's and Yahoo's default), two FLEX (Sleeper's default), three WR or superflex, with or without a kicker and a defense.
I'm in more than one league. What do I need?
One pass per team; one email can hold several. Running the whole league? The League Pass covers every manager in a league of up to 14 teams with one payment.
How is this different from free rankings?
Rankings sort players for everyone. Your report sets your lineup from your roster, under your scoring, and each close call names the bench player and the points gap. Questionable starters get an if/then, byes are checked through the playoffs, and Saturday emails only if Friday's injury report affects your lineup.
What if my roster changes mid-season?
Update it from the link in any report, or reply with it. A change confirmed by Friday night (ET) is in that Saturday's check and every report after.
Can I check how it decides?
Yes. Every call shows the projection behind it and the player's recent games. Each report after your first also lists the calls with odds from the one before it, and how each one finished. How it works has the detail.
Who's behind this?
Independent: one fantasy player built it, after too many lineups set at 12:55 on Sunday. Subscribers alone pay for it: no ads, no sponsors, and your data is never sold. Questions go to hello@beatyourleague.com.
