Tuesday morning, ninety seconds. You open one email. At the top is a three-box checklist — the lineup calls worth making, and the one player whose news you need to check before Sunday. Tick the boxes and you're done.

Everything under the checklist is the evidence for it, there for the weeks you want to argue with us: the odds behind each call, what each starter has actually been given — targets, carries, the ball in his hands — and the week's coin flip decided with the reasoning attached.

Then Monday, last week's calls come back graded against the real box score, with the date they were made still on them. That's the whole routine. It's one email. There's nothing to check, install, or babysit.

Why trust the numbers

We found our own blind spot before you did.

Before launch, we re-played two full seasons of a real league and graded every one of our own start/sit calls against the actual box scores — 2,056 of them. Across all of them we hit 53.5%, and five of six confidence buckets came in wrong. The reason is the most useful thing we learned: we couldn't see who was inactive. That single failure is why the live report refuses to print a confidence number until it has verified both players are active — and why win probability is still withheld entirely.

Fair questions

Before you ask the group chat

Does it work with my league app?
Any of them. We never connect to a league — you paste your roster as plain names, from Sleeper, ESPN, Yahoo, or a napkin. If you can copy it, we can read it.
Does it match my league's scoring?
Yes — you pick PPR, half-PPR, or standard, plus your league size and lineup shape (superflex included). Every number in your file is computed under your settings, because a call under the wrong scoring is somebody else's call.
When does it arrive?
Tuesday morning, every week of the season — after Monday night's box score has been graded, before your lineup decisions are due. The first one lands Tuesday, Sep 8.
What if my roster changes mid-season?
Trades and pickups happen — reply to any report with your updated roster and your file follows it from the next Tuesday. Self-serve roster editing is on the way.
Why is there no win probability on my matchup?
Because we haven't earned it. We only publish a number when our own testing says it holds up, and matchup win odds aren't there yet — same reason a too-close call prints "no call" instead of a guess. We measured that rule against two seasons of real injury reports and published the whole result; the numbers you do see are the ones we're prepared to be graded on, in public.
