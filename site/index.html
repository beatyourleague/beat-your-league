Before you ask the group chat

Does it work with my league?
Yes: ESPN, Yahoo, Sleeper, CBS or any app. Paste your roster, no login. Full PPR, half-PPR or standard (4-point passing touchdowns); 8, 10, 12 or 14 teams; standard (ESPN's and Yahoo's default), two FLEX (Sleeper's default), three WR or superflex lineups, with or without a K and DEF.
Is it worth joining mid-season?
Yes. It's built for the playoff push. Your first report usually lands within the hour, every report checks the byes ahead, playoff weeks included, and Saturday's check catches Friday's injury news.
What if news breaks after Tuesday?
Your report already plans for it: every questionable starter comes with an if/then and the backup who can still play in time. If Friday's injury report changes your lineup, the Saturday check tells you the swap. No news, no email.
What if my roster changes mid-season?
Update it from the link in any report, or reply with it. A change confirmed by Friday night (ET) is in that Saturday's check and every report after. Your roster is used only to build your report and is never sold.
Can I check how it decides?
Yes. Every call shows the projection behind it and the player's recent games. Each report after your first also lists the calls with odds from the one before it, and how each one finished. How it works has the detail.
