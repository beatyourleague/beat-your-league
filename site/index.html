Does it work with my league app?
Any of them — Sleeper, ESPN, Yahoo, anything. You paste your roster once (or pick names from a list) and we take it from there. No league connection needed.
Does it match my league's scoring?
Yes. PPR, half-PPR, or standard, plus your league size and lineup shape (superflex included). Every number is computed under your settings, and each setting — plus the most common combinations of them — was graded against real seasons. Here's how each one did.
When does it arrive?
Tuesday morning, every week of the season — once the whole week's games are in the books, days before your lineup locks. The first one lands the day you sign up.
What if my roster changes mid-season?
Trades and pickups happen — reply to any file with your updated roster and it follows from the next Tuesday.
When do the numbers start?
Right away if you join now: from Week 4 on, every setup gets odds in its first file.
