Why this exists

Before you ask the group chat

Straight answers to what managers ask first.

Does it work with my league app?
Yes — ESPN, Yahoo, Sleeper, CBS or any other. Paste your roster once (or pick names from a list) and we take it from there. No league login, no password.
Does it match my league's scoring?
For the settings most leagues use, yes: full PPR, half-PPR or standard scoring (4-point passing touchdowns), 8 to 14 teams, and a standard, superflex or no-kicker-no-defense lineup. Extras like 6-point passing touchdowns or a TE premium get the nearest standard version, and lineups with two FLEX spots aren't supported yet.
I'm in more than one league. What do I need?
Each team gets its own report and its own subscription, so most managers start with the league that has the most riding on it and add another the same way. One email address can hold several. Running a whole league? The League Pass covers every manager with one payment.
When does it arrive?
Tuesday morning, every week of the season, after Monday night's game and days before your lineup locks. Your first one comes the day you sign up. And if Friday's injury report changes your lineup, a short final check lands Saturday with the swap.
How is this different from free rankings?
Rankings give everyone the same list and leave the decision to you. Your report starts from your roster and your league's scoring, decides every close call, and puts the odds on it — plus an if/then for late news, so Sunday morning isn't a scramble.
My league app already has an “optimize lineup” button. Why this?
That button fills your lineup from the app's projections when you open the app and press it. Your report comes to you on Tuesday with every close call decided and the reason in a line, an if/then for late news, and a check of Friday's injury report on Saturday — so you don't have to remember to look.
Can't I just ask a chatbot or Google?
You can, every week, if you paste in your roster, your scoring and the latest injury news yourself and remember to ask before Sunday. Your report starts from your roster and your league's scoring, works from the real box scores and injury reports, arrives on Tuesday without you asking, and checks Friday's injury report for you on Saturday.
Who's behind this?
Beat Your League is independent and small, built by fantasy players who were tired of setting lineups at 12:55 on Sunday. We're paid only by subscribers: no ads, and we never sell your data. Questions go to hello@beatyourleague.com.
Can I check how it decides?
Yes. Every call shows the projection behind it and the player's recent games, so you can look up the box scores yourself. Every report also lists last week's calls and how each one finished. How each part is worked out is on the How it works page.
How long does it take each week?
About ninety seconds. The top of the report is a short checklist: the lineup to set and the one player whose news to watch. Tick the boxes and you're done — the detail is there if you want it.
What if my roster changes mid-season?
Trades and pickups happen — reply to any report with your updated roster and it follows from the next Tuesday.
When do the numbers start?
Join in Week 4 or later and your first weekly report carries the odds. (Join after that week's first game and you get your roster report the same day, then the week's report on Tuesday.) Early in a season they start sooner or later depending on your setup: Week 1 sets your lineup in last season's order with no odds, full-PPR 12-team standard lineups get them from Week 2, and every other setup from Week 4 (here's what a Week 1 report looks like).
