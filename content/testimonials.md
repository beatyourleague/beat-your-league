# Testimonials — the rules, and the log

Subscribers are asked for one only after a winning week, by one line at the
bottom of The Receipts in their own report: "Good week? Reply and tell us how
it went — we read every one, and with your OK we may quote you." Replies land
in the hello@ inbox.

A quote may go on any page only when ALL of these hold:

1. **It is real and unedited** apart from trimming. No composites, no
   paraphrase presented as a quote, nothing written for them.
2. **They said yes in writing** to that exact wording being published, and to
   the name form (first name + last initial, initials, or anonymous — their
   choice). Keep the reply.
3. **It states what happened, not a promise.** "It told me to start Pollard and
   he went for 24" is fine. Anything implying results for others ("you'll win
   more") is not published, even if they wrote it.
4. **Nothing we didn't see.** If it cites a result, their report for that week
   exists in `reports/subscribers/`.
5. **It comes down on request**, the same day.

No customer counts, star ratings or "trusted by" lines until they are true
and countable.
